      Research · New Energy Nexus Ventures
      SEA Climate Disruptions 2026

      The State of the Industry research I built at New Energy Nexus Ventures explains why Southeast Asia is the next climate-solutions frontier. Up to 11% of regional GDP is at risk by 2100, while annual climate damages already absorb about US$100B. This sits against an US$8T green-economy market cap and multi-trillion-dollar funding gaps. The report maps the full solutions stack (mitigation, adaptation & resilience, and the AI/MRV software backbone) across the region’s integrated laboratory: Singapore’s capital, Indonesia’s demand, Vietnam’s manufacturing, and the Philippines’ resilience.       Research · Living Lab Ventures
      Mid‑Year 2025 VC Outlook
      Full report ↗

      The research publication I co-authored at Living Lab Ventures maps what comes after a bruising start to 2025. Deal value fell 59% across Southeast Asia and 85% in Indonesia from Q4 2024 to Q1 2025, reaching a low not seen since 2022. The report covers healthcare’s climb to the region’s second most-funded sector, manufacturing and nearshoring momentum as global trade reroutes, and the investment windows opened by Prabowo’s economic agenda.
